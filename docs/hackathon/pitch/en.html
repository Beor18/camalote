Appendix · Regulation

We don't hold funds, we don't recommend, and each issuer sets its countries.

Non-custodial

Each user has their own account. Camalote never holds the money and can't withdraw it.

Your rule, not advice

We execute the rule the user sets, on third-party protocols. We don't recommend assets.

Each issuer, its countries

Stocks: not in the US, UK, Canada or Australia. Pre-IPO, Treasuries and loans: not in the US either. Argentina isn't excluded by any of them.

Before investing, every user confirms they don't live in an excluded country; without that, nothing is invested. What's missing: a legal opinion on whether executing the rule requires registering as a PSAV with Argentina's CNV. It's the first thing we fund.
